# YourKhata product demo — storyboard and narration (mobile)

Layout: phone, 390 px wide, touch. Length 5:16. Picture 720x1558. Voice: Hindi, macOS `say`, Lekha at 138 words a minute (female, computer-generated), 0.8s between lines. English is the subtitle track.

Every feature follows one order: the problem and a real moment it bites (a still card), then the answer performed step by step on the real screen, and its benefit in the last line. Recorded by `e2e/demo/record-demo.mjs` on the demo stack; each line is spoken from the moment the recorder begins the step it describes (the timecode beside it).
