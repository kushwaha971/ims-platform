# YourKhata product demo (English) — storyboard and narration (desktop)

Language: English. Layout: desktop. Length 3:10. Picture 1280x720. Voice: macOS `say`, Samantha (female, computer-generated).

Recorded by `e2e/demo/record-demo.mjs` on the demo stack; every action below is performed on the real screen.

## Scene 1: intro (0:00–0:10)

- **On screen:** Title card: the YourKhata mark and the three module names.
- **Recorded actions:** None. A still card.
- **Narration:**
  - This is YourKhata: one place for a small business's records.
  - It has three modules: Shop and billing, Library, and Lending. Let's walk through all three.

## Scene 2: signup (0:10–0:28)

- **On screen:** /signup, then the code step, then business setup (step 1 of 4).
- **Recorded actions:** Type a new mobile number, press Send code, type the 4-digit code, land on business setup; type a business name and an owner name, choose Retail shop.
- **Narration:**
  - Signing up takes only a mobile number. There is no password.
  - Enter your number and press Send code. A four-digit code arrives by SMS.
  - Type the code, and a new number goes straight to business setup.
  - Name your business, choose what kind it is, and continue. It takes about a minute.

## Scene 3: login (0:28–0:43)

- **On screen:** /login, the code step, then the Shop dashboard of Sharma General Store.
- **Recorded actions:** Type the owner's number, Send code, type the code; the dashboard opens. Move across the To collect and To pay tiles, then the menu (the sidebar on a desktop, the bottom bar on a phone).
- **Narration:**
  - Next time, the same number and a fresh code log you back in.
  - The dashboard shows what you have to collect, what you have to pay, today's sales and cash in hand.
  - The menu has customers, bills, purchases, items and reports.

## Scene 4: shop-khata (0:43–0:58)

- **On screen:** Customers, then Ramesh Traders' account, then the You got drawer.
- **Recorded actions:** Open Customers, open Ramesh Traders, press You got, type 500 and a note, save. The balance changes.
- **Narration:**
  - Open Customers to see who owes you. Each customer has an account with a running balance.
  - Here is Ramesh Traders. When he pays, press You got, enter the amount and save.
  - The balance updates at once, and every entry keeps its date and its note.

## Scene 5: shop-bill (0:59–1:13)

- **On screen:** New Invoice, then the issued invoice.
- **Recorded actions:** Choose Party, pick Anita General Store, add Basmati Rice 5kg with quantity 2, watch the tax lines, press Issue and choose Full credit.
- **Narration:**
  - To make a bill, choose the customer and add the items.
  - GST is worked out for you, with CGST and SGST shown separately.
  - Issue the bill and it is ready to print or to share on WhatsApp. Stock comes down by itself.

## Scene 6: shop-stock (1:13–1:25)

- **On screen:** Items, then Reports.
- **Recorded actions:** Open Items and move down the list; open Reports and move across the report names.
- **Narration:**
  - Items shows what is in stock, with tabs for what is low or out.
  - Reports has the day book, receivables aging, the sales register, profit and loss, and a GST summary.

## Scene 7: library-home (1:25–1:40)

- **On screen:** Your businesses, then the Library home screen of Vidya Reading Room.
- **Recorded actions:** Open the business list, choose Vidya Reading Room. The Library home opens: fees, seats by shift, who has to pay.
- **Narration:**
  - Now the Library module, made for libraries and study rooms.
  - One login can hold more than one business, so we simply switch to the reading room.
  - Its home screen shows fees due, how many seats are filled in each shift, and who still has to pay.

## Scene 8: library-students (1:40–1:52)

- **On screen:** Students, then the New admission form.
- **Recorded actions:** Open Students; open New admission, type a name, show the plan, shift and seat fields, then cancel.
- **Narration:**
  - Students lists every member with their plan, their seat and whether the fee is paid.
  - A new admission takes a name, a plan, a shift and a seat, and the fee can be collected on the spot.

## Scene 9: library-books (1:52–2:09)

- **On screen:** Catalogue, then Counter (Issue, then Return).
- **Recorded actions:** Search the catalogue for a title; at the counter type student number M-0004 and book number 9 to issue; switch to Return and type 9 to take it back.
- **Narration:**
  - The catalogue has every book, and how many copies are on the shelf. Search by title, author or book number.
  - At the counter, enter the student's number, then the book's number. The book is issued with its due date.
  - To take a book back, switch to Return and enter the book's number again.

## Scene 10: library-seats (2:09–2:15)

- **On screen:** Seats, then Attendance.
- **Recorded actions:** Open the seat map for the morning shift; open Attendance.
- **Narration:**
  - The seat map shows every seat in each shift, and Attendance marks who came in today.

## Scene 11: lending-today (2:15–2:34)

- **On screen:** Your businesses, then Lending's Today screen of Verma Finance, then the Collect dialog.
- **Recorded actions:** Switch to Verma Finance. On Today, press Collect for the first borrower, choose UPI, save.
- **Narration:**
  - The third module is Lending. It keeps a record of loans and collections. It does not lend or move money itself.
  - Today lists every borrower who is due, in the order of the collection round.
  - Press Collect, choose how it was paid, and save. The day's total moves at once, and a receipt can go out on WhatsApp.

## Scene 12: lending-loans (2:34–2:48)

- **On screen:** Loans, one loan's page, then the New loan form.
- **Recorded actions:** Open Loans; open Salma Begum's loan and scroll through the schedule and collections; open New loan and move down the form.
- **Narration:**
  - Loans shows what is left on each loan, and who is running late.
  - Open a loan for its schedule, every collection, and a statement you can print.
  - A new loan takes the borrower, the amount and the instalments, daily or weekly.

## Scene 13: together (2:48–3:02)

- **On screen:** Your businesses, then Team, then the account menu and the dashboard in Hindi.
- **Recorded actions:** Open the business list showing all three; open Team and Add member; choose Hindi (the account menu on a desktop, the More page on a phone); the screen changes language.
- **Narration:**
  - All three modules share one sign-in. The business switcher moves between them without logging in again.
  - Add your staff by mobile number and give each person a role.
  - And every screen works in Hindi as well as English.

## Scene 14: outro (3:02–3:10)

- **On screen:** Closing card: the YourKhata mark and yourkhata.com.
- **Recorded actions:** None. A still card.
- **Narration:**
  - That is YourKhata: Shop, Library and Lending, in one place.
  - Sign up free with your mobile number at yourkhata.com.
