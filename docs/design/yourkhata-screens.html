The rules behind every screen

A new account sees one next stepNo rows of zeros, no empty charts, no search box over an empty list. One sentence and one button.
Cards on a phone, tables on a computerA phone card shows the name, the one amount that matters, and its status. Everything else is one tap away.
Two words for money direction"You will get" in red and "You will give" in green, the same everywhere. No "owes", "receivable" or "outstanding".
Four different "nothing here" statesNever used yet, nothing matches your search, still loading, and could not load each look and read differently.
The bottom bar staysIt is hidden only while filling a form or inside one customer's khata, where Save or You gave / You got sit at the bottom.
No made-up figuresThe app never shows sample numbers as if they were yours. Sample data appears only in this design page.
